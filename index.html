Start with three habits

Good fact-checking is a process, not a single click

Use these habits whenever a post, headline, image, video, or AI-generated answer seems surprising, emotional, or too convenient.

1

Check the source

Identify who created the information, what expertise they have, and whether the original source can be located.

2

Check the evidence

Look for documents, data, direct quotations, images, and other evidence that actually support the claim.

3

Check beyond AI

AI can suggest questions and search terms, but important claims should be verified with primary sources and credible independent reporting.
